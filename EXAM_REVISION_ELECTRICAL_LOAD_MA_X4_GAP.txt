================================================================================
          EXAMINATION REVISION SHEET: ELECTRICAL LOAD DATASET
     Topic: Interpretation of the Gap between DEMAND and MA_X-4
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS MA_X-4?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Definition: 
  MA_X-4 is the 4-week moving average (Moving Average of Lag X-4).
  It represents the average electrical demand for that EXACT same day of the 
  week and EXACT same hour over the preceding 4 weeks:
  
      MA_X-4 = (week_X-1 + week_X-2 + week_X-3 + week_X-4) / 4

- Analogy:
  Think of MA_X-4 as the "routine habit" or "expected baseline" of the power grid.
  DEMAND is what consumers "actually consumed" in real-time.

- Under normal conditions:
  Human electricity consumption follows strict weekly and hourly cycles.
  Therefore, MA_X-4 and DEMAND have an extremely high correlation (r > 0.99)
  and track each other almost identically.


2. WHAT DOES A REALLY BIG GAP MEAN?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If |DEMAND - MA_X-4| is very large, an EXCEPTIONAL REAL-WORLD EVENT occurred.
The gap serves as an automatic anomaly detector for the grid.


3. THE TWO SCENARIOS WITH REAL EXAMPLES FROM OUR DATASET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[A] HUGE NEGATIVE GAP: DEMAND << MA_X-4 (Grid Lost Power / Drop in Load)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Meaning:
  The electrical grid consumed far less power than expected by the 4-week routine.

- Concrete Example from our Dataset:
  * Date & Hour: January 20, 2019 at 12:00:00
  * Expected (MA_X-4): 1,176.2 MW
  * Actual DEMAND:        85.2 MW
  * GAP:               -1,091.0 MW  (-93% drop!)
  * Progression:
      12:00 ->  85.2 MW
      13:00 -> 135.8 MW
      14:00 -> 263.6 MW
      15:00 -> 417.4 MW
  * Interpretation:
    At 12:00, electricity collapsed by over 90%, followed by gradual hourly recovery.
    This is the textbook signature of a MAJOR REGIONAL BLACKOUT / OUTAGE and
    step-by-step phased restoration.

- Other Causes:
  * Public holidays (factories, commercial towers, and schools closed).
  * Emergency industrial maintenance or nationwide strikes.
  * Sudden shift to mild weather (no air conditioning needed).


[B] HUGE POSITIVE GAP: DEMAND >> MA_X-4 (Unexpected Demand Surge)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Meaning:
  The grid consumed significantly more power than the 4-week historical baseline.

- Concrete Example from our Dataset:
  * Date & Hour: January 22, 2016 at 13:00:00 (also July 25, 2017)
  * Expected (MA_X-4): ~1,165 MW
  * Actual DEMAND:     ~1,481 MW
  * GAP:                  +315 MW (+27% surge!)
  * Temperature (T2M_toc): Exceeded 30.5 °C
  * Interpretation:
    A sudden severe heatwave hit the region. Thousands of air conditioners
    turned on simultaneously at maximum power, driving an unexpected peak surge.

- Other Causes:
  * Cold load pickup right after power restoration (appliances pulling surge current).
  * Major synchronized cultural or national live events (televised finals, festivals).


4. QUICK EXAM TALKING POINTS (30-SECOND SUMMARY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Baseline: MA_X-4 is the 4-week routine demand for that hour; DEMAND is reality.
2. Correlation: They normally correlate at r > 0.99.
3. Negative Spikes: Indicate blackouts, grid trips, or public holidays.
4. Positive Spikes: Indicate extreme heatwaves (HVAC spikes) or cold load surges.
